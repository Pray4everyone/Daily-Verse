What to check

  1. Confirm that a verse, reference, and feed date appear above.
  2. Choose Load daily audio. Confirm the player is visible and you can hear the recording without leaving this page.
  3. Try Psalm 23, then John 3. Confirm the chapter changes and the previous recording stops.
  4. Return to Load daily audio. Repeat on your phone, including turning the phone sideways.
  5. Open this page again tomorrow and check the feed date, verse, and audio. The feed’s day may change at a different time from your local midnight.
